Charge pump LN8000: delivery readiness

Xiaomi Pad 5 (nabu) · Windows on ARM64 · driver ln8000_kmdf.sys · 2026-09-16

The charge pump driver is built for ARM64, signed and packaged. The chip control logic is ported one-to-one from the GPL Android driver, covered by tests and wired into the kernel driver through the I²C bus of the PEIC node. Below - what exactly is checked mechanically, what requires the tablet, and how to roll back.

Every step of the path is cross-checked against a source: the address and modes against ln8000_charger.c/.h, the bus path against reshub.h, the transfer list against spb.h, the node structure against a live DSDT dump of the tablet.

Limits and honest caveats

The physical check has not been performed. The ten-point acceptance protocol is prepared (below) but not filled with measurements: that needs a tablet with the driver installed and an external USB multimeter. Everything that can be checked without hardware is checked and confirmed by commands.

9/11 V negotiation is not the charge pump's job. The increased voltage with the power brick is negotiated by the Type-C/PD part of the platform (PM8150B). Without it LN8000 runs in 1:1 bypass from 5 V or holds the already negotiated voltage. This is recorded in the project roadmap.

All seven control codes are implemented. State, register read and write, limits, mode, sessions and sample dump - no stubs. The applied current and mode are returned as the chip confirmed them, not as the client requested them.

Acceptance protocol on the device

The run is automated: on the tablet it is enough to execute run-acceptance.ps1 - it walks through the points, captures driver telemetry, collects multimeter readings and generates the protocol itself in HTML, Markdown and JSON with the journal attached. The -DryRun mode checks the pipeline without hardware and marks the report as not verified.

Risks and rollback

RiskRatingMitigation
Overheating in accelerated modemediumthree protection levels: current reduction (43 °C) -> bypass (48 °C) -> stop (55 °C), every event in the journal
False ADC readingslowa decision from three channels, thresholds with margin, every write is verified by a read
No bus responsemediuma 1 s timeout, two attempts, on failure bypass/standby and a journal entry
Incorrect register addressmediumthe addresses are cross-checked against the GPL driver, values are verified by read-back

Rollback: uninstall-driver.ps1 stops the service and removes the package, keeping a transcript in %ProgramData%\nabu-fastcharge\uninstall.log. The driver does not write to the firmware and does not change power settings - removal returns the device to its pre-installation behavior. The previous version is saved in %ProgramData%\nabu-fastcharge\backup and restored with one pnputil command.
